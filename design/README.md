# Design-process templates

`templates.xlsx` is generated by `build_templates.py` (openpyxl) and committed. To regenerate it:

```bash
python design/build_templates.py            # overwrites design/templates.xlsx
```

**Every judgment in this workbook is the team's to make.** The generator fills in only facts
from `docs/plan.md`: criteria names and targets, constraints, the candidate concepts, the
functions to analyse, and the budget figures. Each comparison, relationship, rating, score and
threshold starts **empty and lightly shaded (yellow)**. Formulas fill in as you type. Each sheet
has an instructions row at the top.

| Sheet | What you enter | What the formulas do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pairwise** | Upper triangle: 1 (row more important), 0.5 (equal), 0 (less) for the eight criteria | Lower triangle = 1 - mirror; row totals; normalised weights (feed the next sheets); count of comparisons entered (of 28) |
| **HouseOfQuality** | Relationship strength 9/3/1 between customer needs and nine engineering characteristics; importance of the four constraint rows (optional); the roof correlations (++ + - --) | Criterion importance pulled from Pairwise; technical importance = SUMPRODUCT per column; relative importance and rank |
| **Pugh** | Datum concept (dropdown); + / S / - for each other concept and criterion | Datum column greyed; COUNTIF counts of +, S and -; net score and rank |
| **WeightedMatrix** | Scores from 1 to 5 per concept and criterion | Weights linked from Pairwise; weighted total (max 5); rank; scores-entered counter |
| **FMEA** | Failure modes, effects, causes, controls; S, O, D from 1 to 10; action-priority thresholds; actions and re-ratings | RPN = S x O x D (before and after); action-priority flag HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW from the team's thresholds |
| **BOM** | Item, part number, supplier, qty, unit cost (CAD), per-printer Y/N, link | Extended cost; totals; per-printer subtotal; checks against the $385 project allowance and the under-$400 per-printer criterion, coloured green (OK) or red (OVER) |

Notes:

- Concepts: filament motion, vision, vibration, motor current, acoustic emission, extrusion
  force, heater power. The plan reserves the camera for labels and verification, and lists no
  acoustic-emission sensor. Both are still included so the screening records why they were
  kept or dropped.
- The pairwise method can give the least important criterion a weight of zero. Decide whether
  to accept that, or add a constant to each row total before normalising.
- The FMEA columns follow the IEC 60812 worksheet: function, failure mode, effect, S, cause,
  prevention, O, detection, D, RPN, action, responsibility, action taken, re-rated S/O/D/RPN.
  The action-priority flag is a simple threshold rule, not the AIAG-VDA AP table. Replace it if
  the course requires that table.
- Budget: the proposal states both $385 ($350 plus 10%) and $1,200. The BOM uses $385. Edit
  cell C33 once the budget is confirmed.
- Formulas were checked by recalculating a filled-in copy in LibreOffice (no errors). openpyxl
  stores formulas without cached values, so a viewer that does not recalculate shows blanks
  until the file is opened and saved in Excel or LibreOffice.
